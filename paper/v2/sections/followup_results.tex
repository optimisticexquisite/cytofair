\paragraph{CRIC models do not apply a slide-level label prior (F1).} Over \FoneCells\ discordant
cells from \FoneSlides\ slides, the probability of the slide's majority class was
\FoneEst~pp \emph{lower}, not higher, when the cell's slide was in training (\FoneLo\ to
\FoneHi; F1 \FoneSupported). Crop-level training improved accuracy as much for cells whose class
disagreed with their slide's majority (\FoneDiscordant~pp) as for cells that agreed
(\FoneConcordant~pp; contrast \FoneContrast~pp, \FoneContrastLo\ to \FoneContrastHi). Whatever
the models learn from seen slides helps each cell toward its own class rather than toward the
slide's typical class.

\paragraph{Carriers at test time (F2).} Stain transfer changed the leak advantage by
\FtwoEst~pp (\FtwoLo\ to \FtwoHi; F2 \FtwoSupported). The corresponding reductions were
\AblGreyEst~pp for greyscale (\AblGreyLo\ to \AblGreyHi), \AblLowEst~pp for low-pass filtering
(\AblLowLo\ to \AblLowHi), \AblFlatEst~pp for illumination flattening (\AblFlatLo\ to
\AblFlatHi), and \AblBgEst~pp for removing the background (\AblBgLo\ to \AblBgHi).
Grad-CAM attribution outside the cell mask differed between leak and clean models by
\CamLeakEst~pp (\CamLeakLo\ to \CamLeakHi; descriptive).

\paragraph{Carriers in training (F3, F4).} On fresh seeds the standard recipe replicated H3
(\HthreeRepEst~pp, \HthreeRepLo\ to \HthreeRepHi). With every background masked in training
and testing the leak effect was \FthreeEst~pp (\FthreeLo\ to \FthreeHi; F3 \FthreeSupported);
masking reduced it by \MaskReductionEst~pp relative to the standard recipe
(\MaskReductionLo\ to \MaskReductionHi). Colour augmentation reduced it by \FfourEst~pp
(\FfourLo\ to \FfourHi; F4 \FfourSupported).

\paragraph{Dose-response (F5--F8).} Erasing within-class parent identity reduced the gap by
\FfiveEst~pp at the strongest in-regime rank (\FfiveLo\ to \FfiveHi; F5 \FfiveSupported). Each
unit of normalisation strength reduced it by \FsixEst~pp (\FsixLo\ to \FsixHi; F6
\FsixSupported). Under the probe-matched adversary the slope of gap on recoverability was
\FsevenEst\ (\FsevenLo\ to \FsevenHi; F7 \FsevenSupported), and pooled over all included
manipulations \FeightEst\ (\FeightLo\ to \FeightHi; F8 \FeightSupported).
